# Bolas Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

Bolas is where people find others to build with. Keep it the kind of place you'd want to show up
to. These guidelines are part of our [Terms]({{SITE}}/terms), and we don't tolerate
objectionable content or abusive behavior.

## Be real

- Use your own name and photo. One account per person.
- Say what you're actually building, looking for, and good at.
- You must be 18 or older.

## Don't sell to strangers

- No pitching products, courses, or services to people who didn't ask.
- No "passive income," crypto signals, MLMs, or "DM me to learn how" posts.
- Never ask someone for money, gift cards, or payment details.

## Be respectful

- No harassment, bullying, threats, or unwanted advances, in the app or in person.
- No hate speech, slurs, or attacks on anyone's identity.
- Take "no" for an answer. If someone doesn't reply, move on.

## Keep it appropriate

- No sexual content or nudity, and nothing involving minors, ever.
- No violence, gore, or content that encourages self-harm.
- No illegal activity.

## Respect privacy

- Don't share other people's personal information, photos, or messages without permission.
- Don't share an event's exact location with people who aren't going.
- Don't use Bolas to track or find anyone who doesn't want to be found.

## Meet safely

- Meet in public places, especially the first time.
- Read our [Safety Tips]({{SITE}}/safety) before meeting someone new.

## Hosting events

- Host in public places, and describe your event honestly.
- Don't use events to sell things or to collect people's information.

## Report and block

- If something feels off, **report it**. Tap **⋯** on a profile, long-press a message, or tap
  **Report** after meeting someone in person. Reports are private, and we review them within
  24 hours.
- **Block** anyone you don't want to hear from. They won't be told.

## What happens if you break these rules

Depending on what happened, we may remove content, limit features, or suspend or delete accounts,
sometimes without warning. We report illegal activity, including anything involving the
exploitation of minors, to the authorities.
