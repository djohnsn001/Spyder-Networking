# Bolas Safety Tips

Most people on Bolas are here to build something, just like you. But Bolas doesn't run background
checks or verify identities, so look out for yourself when you meet someone new.

## Before you meet

- **Get to know them a little first.** Look at their profile, and whether you have mutual
  connections. A warm intro through someone you both know is the best start.
- **Keep chatting in Bolas** until you're comfortable. Hold off on sharing your phone number,
  social accounts, or workplace.
- **Never share financial information** or send money, gift cards, or crypto, no matter the
  story. That's the classic sign of a scam.
- **Don't share your home address.**

## When you meet

- **Meet in a public place** with other people around: a coffee shop, a campus space, a coworking
  space, or a public event.
- **Tell a friend** where you're going, who you're meeting, and when you expect to be done.
- **Arrange your own transportation** so you can leave whenever you want.
- **Keep your phone charged**, and stay in control of your drinks and belongings.
- **Trust your instincts.** If something feels wrong, leave. You don't owe anyone an explanation.

## At events

- Events on Bolas are hosted by other users, not by Bolas.
- The exact location only unlocks after you tap **Going**. Don't share it with people who aren't
  attending.
- Prefer events in public places. If a location or description seems off, **report the event**
  (tap **⋯** on the event).

## Your location

- **Show me on the map** is off unless you turn it on. When it's on, only people you've met in
  person see your approximate area, never your exact spot.
- You can turn it off any time in **Settings**, and your location is deleted right away.

## If something goes wrong

- **In an emergency, call 911.**
- **Report the person in Bolas.** Tap **⋯** on their profile, long-press a message they sent, or
  tap **Report** on the screen that appears after you connect in person. Reports are private, and
  we review them within 24 hours.
- **Block them.** They won't be able to find you, message you, or connect with you, and they won't
  be told.
- If you think someone on Bolas is **under 18**, report them.
- For anything else, email us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
